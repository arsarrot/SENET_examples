Abstract

A convolutional denoising auto-encoder (CDAE) for the encoding and decoding of the signal. The CDAE contains an enhancement layer in its bottleneck that consists of interleaved Grouped-DPRNN and DPCNNBlocks. A DPCNNBlock consists of one or more Dual-path convolution neural networks (DPCNNs), and DPCNN comprises a number of convolution modules used for time-frequency information processing in dual paths. The convolution module is inspired by the design of the convolution module used in Conformer to augment the modeling capability of the transformer. .
